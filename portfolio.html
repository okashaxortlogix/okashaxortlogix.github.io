About

I'm an AI Engineer at Xort Logix in Islamabad, and I build end-to-end AI systems that work outside the lab — from an IEEE-published medical imaging model and a real-time assistive headset for the visually impaired, to enterprise RAG assistants and autonomous conversation agents in production use.

My flagship project, CHASHM AI, runs a YOLOv8 detection model on an ESP32-CAM headset — 94.2% mAP at 45 ms latency — announcing people, vehicles and obstacles to the wearer through speech. At Xort Logix I built the GoHighLevel RAG assistant over 5,717 indexed chunks, and my IEEE-published chicken pox detection model reached 97.31% accuracy with ConvNeXt.

I also build the complete products around the models: DastKar Hub, a marketplace with 325 products from 50 verified artisan studios, alongside hospital ERPs, publishing platforms and security tooling — shipped with React, Next.js, Laravel and FastAPI.
